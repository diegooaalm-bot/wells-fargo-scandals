Root causeAggressive sales targets and compensation incentives encouraged misconduct.

Key takeaway

It wasn’t just unethical employees. It was the system.

  1. 01It rewarded the wrong behaviors.
  2. 02It pushed employees to prioritize unrealistic goals.
  3. 03It discouraged them from challenging unethical practices.

Class concept 1 · How it works

Hoping for A.
Rewarding B.

Reinforcement theory. Employees learned that meeting sales targets brought rewards, while missing them could bring negative consequences, regardless of customer benefit.

The result · Misaligned behaviorEmployees opened unauthorized accounts to achieve targets, undermining customer trust.

Class concept 2 · Downsides of goal setting

Aggressive goals create tunnel vision.

Tunnel vision · Narrowing focus
Aggressive sales quotas shifted attention toward account openings rather than customers’ actual financial needs.
Unethical behavior · Manipulating performance
Some employees opened unauthorized accounts and transferred funds to meet quotas or avoid consequences.

Aggressive goals backfire when achieving the target matters more than how it is achieved.

Class concept 3 · Ethics & psychological safety

Speaking up was punished.

  • 2007–2016Some reported fake account practices. Those who raised concerns faced retaliation.
  • 2017Fired after reporting suspected banking violations. OSHA ordered reinstatement.
  • 2022Senior manager fired after reporting misconduct. OSHA ordered over $22M in back pay and damages.

Why now

Fifteen years of scandals. Still no safe way to speak up.

Employees may remain silent after years of whistleblower retaliation, and Wells Fargo currently lacks a safe system for reporting ethical concerns.

Solution 1 · Reinforcement theory

Reward what you hope for.

  • Find integrative solutionsBalance growth with customer needs. Reward satisfaction alongside results.
  • Link goals to strategyConnect each role to customer trust. Evaluate service, compliance and performance.
  • Get employee participationInvolve employees in setting targets. Use feedback to keep expectations realistic.

Solution 2 · Goal setting

Set SMARTer goals for new accounts.

100%
Proposed target Authentication rate for newly opened accounts, by the end of 2027.

Solution 3 · Deontological framework

Define right and wrong.

Promote a deontological framework, where right and wrong are clearly defined, so the company avoids the slippery slope of unethical behavior.

Solution 4 · Psychological safety

Appoint an Ethics Captain.

Every employee gets a designated Ethics Captain: an independent, anonymous and easy way to report concerns.

  • IndependentDistance from the functional group promotes honest feedback.
  • AnonymousAnonymity avoids individual retaliation.
  • AccessibleReporting takes less commitment.
  • AvailableRegular emails keep the resource top of mind.
